Net Atlas Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Net Atlas ("the app") is published by Code Compiler. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no location permission, and your history and monitor list never leave your phone.

1. Information the app sends

Net Atlas is a set of network tools. Each tool only sends what it needs to answer the check you start:

  • DNS Lookup and DNS Propagation: the domain or IP you type and the record type are sent over DNS-over-HTTPS to the public resolver you choose, Google Public DNS (dns.google) or Cloudflare DNS (cloudflare-dns.com). DNS Propagation also sends the same query to AdGuard DNS, NextDNS and Alibaba AliDNS to compare the answers.
  • WHOIS / Domain info: the domain or IP you type is sent to rdap.org, which forwards the request to the registry or regional internet registry responsible for it.
  • My IP: when you open My IP, the app sends a request to ip-api.com. Like every internet request, it carries your IP address. ip-api.com returns your public IP, internet provider, ASN and an approximate (city-level) location. The free ip-api.com service is reached over a standard (HTTP) connection, which is not encrypted. ip-api.com's handling of requests is described in its own policy: ip-api.com/docs/legal.
  • SSL Certificate Checker, HTTP Headers & Redirects, Port Checker, TCP Ping and Uptime Monitor: the app connects directly to the host, URL or port you choose. That host sees your device's IP address, as with any connection your browser makes.

These services receive the request only to return the result to you. The app uses the answers only to display them on your screen. Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy.

2. Information stored on your device

  • History: your past lookups (and any hosts you pin) are saved only on your phone so you can view or re-run them. You can switch History off or clear it in Settings.
  • Uptime Monitor list: the websites you add (up to 10) and their last status are saved only on your phone. The app checks them in the background about every 15 minutes and shows a notification if a site goes down, comes back up, or its SSL certificate expires within 14 days. You can remove any monitor at any time.
  • Settings: your theme, chosen DNS resolver and History preference are saved on your phone.

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

3. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to run the checks and to tell you when you are offline.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and newer): asked only when you add your first Uptime monitor, and used only to alert you when a website you added goes down or its SSL certificate is about to expire.
  • Run at startup and keep awake: added by Android's WorkManager so your monitors keep working after the phone restarts.

4. What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for or use location permission or GPS.
  • It does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or files.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • It does not sell or rent any information.
  • We (the developer) do not run any server that receives your data.

5. Sharing

Information is only sent to the services and hosts described above, to provide the feature you use. When you use the app's Share or Export button, the text goes to the app you pick, under your control.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Accuracy and responsible use

IP location shows where a network is registered, usually the city or region of an internet provider. It is not a street address and must not be used to identify or locate a person. Port Checker and TCP Ping check only the host and ports you enter; please use them only on systems you own or are allowed to test.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
